AI Story Book Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-02-20

1. Data We Collect

  • Service identifier (X-User-Key)
  • Story generation inputs (topic, age band, style)
  • Photos for character generation (optional)

2. Children's Data

Children-related data is processed only after parental consent. Parents can revoke consent and request deletion in Settings.

3. Photo Processing

Uploaded photos are used only for character generation and can be deleted upon request.

4. Retention and Deletion

When users request "Delete all my data," related records are removed.
