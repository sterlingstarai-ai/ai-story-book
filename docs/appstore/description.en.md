# AI Story Book (English Description)

Create personalized AI storybooks for your child. New story generation in the free beta is available to invited accounts.

## Key Features
- Choose a topic and illustration style, and compare sample passages for ages 3–5, 5–7, and 7–9 before creating a story.
- Read and save stories in your library
- Korean narration for the free first book in every supported age band, with storyteller, animated, and bedtime styles.
- Parent-first safety: parental consent, age gate, screen-time controls
- Reading activity: reading stats and streak calendar

## Free Beta
- Invited new accounts can create their first illustrated storybook for free. The Create screen guides families to the beta invitation ID in Settings when an invitation is needed.
- New subscriptions and credit purchases are unavailable.
- Generating new stories and narration is subject to account and service-wide usage limits.
- Save a recovery code from parent settings before reinstalling the app or changing devices to restore your library. Recovery is unavailable if you lose both device access and the recovery code.
- Existing users can still view credits and restore eligible store purchases.

## Privacy & Safety
- Consent-based data processing for child-focused use
- In-app account data deletion support
- Refer to Privacy Policy and Terms for full details
